About

Hands-on troubleshooting, explained in plain language

I'm an entry-level IT support candidate with a CompTIA Security+ certification and an Associate of Science in Cybersecurity (Microsoft specialization) from Chattahoochee Technical College. I troubleshoot Windows 10/11, repair hardware, and resolve Microsoft 365, printer, and network issues, and I'm currently building Active Directory environments in a home lab to practice real administration work.
