Chapter I

The Origin

Ω

Every hero has an origin, and every origin begins with curiosity. Mine started with a simple question — what happens if I push here? — and grew into a discipline: finding the cracks in systems before someone with worse intentions does.

I'm David Novis, a security researcher and engineer who treats every engagement like a labor worthy of the old myths — methodical, relentless, and a little theatrical about it. My work spans offensive security, defensive hardening, and the write-ups that turn both into lessons worth sharing.

"Know thy attack surface, as thou wouldst know thyself."
